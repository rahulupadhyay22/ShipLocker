Before Creating a Shipment

  • - Ensure all intended items are in Ready to Ship and included in your request. Changes after boxing incur a ₹2000 repacking fee.
  • - Heavier shipments = lower rates, so consolidate items where possible.
  • - Packing requires 24–48 hours.
  • - Import duties are the receiver's responsibility.

Documents Needed to Ship Internationally - IMPORTANT

For KYC, please provide an Aadhaar Card (yours/relative's/friend's). Without it, the shipment will move in "Commercial Mode" under IndiaBox, leading to extra scrutiny, higher duties, and longer processing times.
