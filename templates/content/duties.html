Duties & Customs Information

Understand import duties, taxes, and customs procedures for international shipments.

Duties Are Receiver Responsibility

Import duties are excluded & assessed by destination customs based on item value. Tariff is emailed to the receiver. CamelTrunk is not liable for rejected shipments.

Key Points

  • Duties are payable by the receiver - The person receiving the package pays any applicable customs duties.
  • Duty rates vary by country - Each country has its own duty rates based on product category and value.
  • De minimis thresholds - Many countries have a minimum value below which no duties apply.
  • Commercial vs Personal - Commercial shipments may have different duty rates than personal ones.

Common Destination Duty Rates

Approximate duty rates (may vary based on product category):

  • USA: 0-25% (de minimis $800)
  • UK: 0-20% VAT + duties (de minimis £135)
  • Canada: 0-18% + GST (de minimis $20 CAD)
  • Australia: 5-10% + 10% GST (de minimis $1000 AUD)
  • UAE: 5% VAT (de minimis varies)
  • Singapore: 7% GST (de minimis $400 SGD)

* Rates are approximate and subject to change. Always check with local customs.

🇺🇸 U.S. IEEPA Tariff Updates

Effective August 2025, shipments from India to the USA are subject to additional IEEPA tariffs:

  • Additional IEEPA rate: 25%
  • Total IEEPA duty: 50% (25% + 25%) + general duty rate
  • Expect extended clearance delays

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if I don't pay duties?
The shipment will be held at customs and may be returned to origin or destroyed. You'll also be charged return shipping fees.
Can I prepay duties?
Some carriers offer Delivered Duty Paid (DDP) options. Contact us for more information about DDP shipping.
Why is my package stuck at customs?
Packages may be held for documentation review, duty payment, or random inspection. Check your email for any customs notices.
How long does customs clearance take?
Typically 1-5 business days, but can be longer during peak seasons or if additional documentation is required.
