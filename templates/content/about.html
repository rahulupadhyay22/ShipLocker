Why CamelTrunk?

Founded by a team of logistics experts and tech enthusiasts, CamelTrunk was born out of a personal frustration: the difficulty of getting authentic Indian products abroad. High shipping costs, customs complexity, and unreliable couriers made it a nightmare.

We built CamelTrunk to fix this. By consolidating shipments, we drastically reduce costs. By handling customs paperwork, we remove the stress. And by using technology, we give you complete control over your packages.
