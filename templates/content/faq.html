Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about shopping in India and shipping globally.

General

INDIBOX provides you with a free virtual address in India. You can use this address to shop from any Indian online store (like Amazon India, Myntra, Flipkart). We receive your packages, consolidate them into one shipment, and forward them to your doorstep anywhere in the world.

It's completely free to sign up and get your Indian address! There are no monthly membership fees. You only pay for shipping and any optional services you request.

Shipping & Consolidation

Consolidation is the process of combining multiple packages into a single shipment. International shipping carriers charge a base rate for the first 0.5kg. By combining packages, you only pay this base rate once, which can save you up to 80% compared to shipping each package individually.

International shipping is charged based on the higher of Actual Weight or Volumetric Weight. Volumetric weight is calculated as (Length x Width x Height in cm) / 5000. Our teams always try to repack items to minimize volumetric weight.

Storage & Services
