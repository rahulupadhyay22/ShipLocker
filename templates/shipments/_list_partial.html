International Shipments

CamelTrunk is not responsible for delivery delays, customs clearance, VAT, duties, taxes, or shipments rejected/returned to India.

Once a shipment has been handed over to the carrier, the receiver must work directly with the carrier to clear customs, pay duties/taxes, and ensure successful delivery.

After shipping, it is the receiver's responsibility to follow up with the carrier until the package is delivered to their doorstep.

👉 If any additional documents, customs certifications, or declarations are required, the receiver can connect with CamelTrunk Support for assistance and we can help procure the documents if its possible.
ⓘ Package is stuck at customs?

After shipping, you are responsible for following up directly with the carrier to arrange customs clearance, shipment release, and ensure delivery is completed.

Domestic Within India Shipments

Pay service charges, then schedule pickup from your locker address (10 AM - 5 PM IST) using any courier of your choice.

  1. Once service charges are paid, you will need to schedule pickup from Locker address.
  2. Pickup Address: {{ request.user.locker.locker_id }} - {{ request.user.locker.address|default:"Locker Address" }}
  3. Pickup Time: 10 AM to 5 PM IST
  4. Schedule pickup using any available courier service in India, some examples:
    • Premium - DTDC, BlueDart
    • Low Cost - Delhivery
    • Within Hyderabad - Rapido
CamelTrunk is not affiliated with or responsible for any third-party courier services. Customers may use any domestic courier of their choice to send or collect packages. The links provided are for convenience only, and CamelTrunk bears no liability for the performance or conduct of these services.
5. Share pickup labels with CamelTrunk team. We'll ensure your package is ready for handover at the scheduled time.

You are responsible for coordinating pickup with your chosen service provider during business hours (10 AM - 5 PM) and ensure smooth delivery of the package to India address.
