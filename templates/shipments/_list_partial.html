International Shipments

Ruffleberry is not responsible for delivery delays, customs clearance, VAT, duties, or taxes.

Ruffleberry is also not responsible for shipments that are rejected, destroyed, or returned to India.

Once a shipment has been handed over to the carrier, the receiver must work directly with the carrier to clear customs, pay duties/taxes, and ensure successful delivery.

After shipping, it is the receiver's responsibility to follow up with the carrier until the package is delivered to their doorstep.

👉 If any additional documents, customs certifications, or declarations are required, the receiver can connect with Ruffleberry Support for assistance and we can help procure the documents if its possible.
ⓘ Package is stuck at customs?

After shipping, you are responsible for following up directly with the carrier to arrange customs clearance, shipment release, and ensure delivery is completed.
