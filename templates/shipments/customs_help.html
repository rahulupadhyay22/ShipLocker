📋 Step-by-Step Resolution

  1. Check your tracking status

    Visit the carrier website and enter your tracking number to see the current status.

  2. Contact the carrier directly

    Call the helpline numbers above. Have your tracking number ready.

  3. Provide required documents

    Customs may require: Commercial Invoice, Aadhaar/ID, Proof of payment.

  4. Pay applicable duties

    Import duties are the receiver's responsibility. Pay promptly to avoid delays.

  5. Schedule redelivery

    Once cleared, arrange delivery with the carrier.
